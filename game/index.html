EMBER COURT
A turn-based card battler for the throne of cinders
Goal: reduce the Ash Regent to 0 life before yours reaches 0. Both sides start at 30 life.
Each turn: gain mana crystals (1 on turn 1, up to 10), draw a card, then play cards by clicking them. A card costs mana equal to its blue number.
Creatures stay on the table and attack with their red number; green is their health. Guards must be attacked before anything else. New creatures rest one turn.
Spells strike at once: Ember Bolt burns, Wildfire scorches every enemy creature, Healing Spring mends you, Ember Wisdom draws cards.
Suits: Ember hits hard, Thorn guards, Tide heals and draws, Stone endures. Mix them to win.
Mouse: click a card, then click its target. Keyboard: 1-7 select a hand card, Enter ends the turn, R restarts after a win or loss.
